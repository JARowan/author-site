About J.A. Rowan

J.A. Rowan writes from the edge of memory and salt air. A native son of Hawaiʻi now rooted in the high plains, he carries stories of land, love, and legacy wherever he goes.

His work walks the tension between the sacred and the seductive, always returning to the question: what does it mean to belong — to a person, a place, a self?
